The importance of learning DevOps engineering in software development cycle. 

DevOps for System administrators is important because it enhances your ability to manage and configure server infrastructure.  This allows for automation and streamlining of software develop and deployment processes.  This results in faster software delivery, improved collaboration and better scalability.  This helps to build reliable systems where the infrastructure can support the demands of the software development lifecycle.  

DevOps bridges the gap between development and operations teams which enables faster, more reliable and automated software delivery.  

Key Reasons to Learn DevOps:
- Faster release cycles and efficiency.
- Improved Collaboration
- Automation and Reduced Errors
- High Job Demand and Security
- Better Product Reliability
- Continuous Learning and Growth
