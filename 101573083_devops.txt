This file is about George Brown College's COMP 2156 DevOps course. 

It is a required course for the T191 Computer Systems Technician students. 

The current term is Winter 2026 and is being taught by Pritesh Patel.

Classes are held online through Teams. 
Lectures are on Mondays from 6-8pm.
Labs are on Wednesdays from 4-6pm. 

Labs are presented each week and the corresponding screenshots are due by the end of day on Sunday.  
8 out of 10 labs are marked and represent 16% of the Final Grade.
