George Brown Polytechnic
George Brown Polytechnic, formely known as George Brown College, is one of Canada's premier colleges, located in the heart of downtown Toronto, Ontario, named after Globe founder George Brown. It operates from the three of its main campuses, Casa Loma, St. James and Waterfront. The college specializes in applied, hands on learning, covering a plethora technical skills for many different career fields. 
The main specializations for the three campuses are: technology, engineering, construction and fashion for Casa Loma. Business, culinary arts and community services for St. James and health sciences and community services for Waterfront.
