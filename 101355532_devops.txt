This is some information about the COMP2156 DevOps course at George Brown
The COMP2156 DevOps course at George Brown covers many development areas, such as DevOps lifecycles, CI/CD pipelines and version management.
COMP2156 is a self-directed online course, and was designed and developed from a partnership between George Brown College and Arcitura
 The course aids students in learning essential skillsets required for a career in development, with course content teaching crucial knowledge, such as DevOps lifecycles, continuous intergration and delivery and deployment pipelines.
